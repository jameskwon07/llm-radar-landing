## What It Does

Most brands are invisible in AI search — and they don't even know it.

When someone asks Perplexity "recommend a tool for X" or "what is [your brand]?", is your brand mentioned? LLM Radar answers that question in 30 seconds.

**How it works:**

1. Enter your URL
2. LLM Radar generates relevant questions about your brand and category
3. It queries Perplexity for each question
4. It detects whether your brand is mentioned — and shows the actual AI response

---

## Scan Structure

LLM Radar runs a 3-level scan:

| Level | Type | Example |
|-------|------|---------|
| **Brand questions** | Directly asks about your brand | "What is [Brand]?" / "Anyone tried [Brand]?" |
| **Category questions** | Asks about your category without naming you | "Recommend an AI monitoring tool" |
| **General questions** | Broad industry questions | "How do I improve AI search visibility?" |

Category-level visibility is harder to achieve — and more valuable, because it reaches people who've never heard of you.
